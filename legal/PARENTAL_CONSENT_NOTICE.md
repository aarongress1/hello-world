## Before your child starts — a quick, important note for parents

CurioKids is a learning companion for children in grades K–8. Because your child
is under 13, U.S. law (**COPPA**) requires us to tell you what we collect and get
your permission first. Please read this before you consent.

### 1. You are in control
You — the parent or legal guardian — create and control this account. **Your
child does not create an account, log in, or enter personal information.** You set
up each child profile and choose how much oversight you want.

### 2. What we will collect from your child
- The child's **first name (or a nickname)**, **grade**, and **interests** — all
  entered by **you**.
- The **conversations** your child has with the CurioKids guide.
- **Learning-plan progress** and **safety flags** (so we can show you a debrief
  and alert you to anything concerning).

We collect the **minimum** needed. We do **not** collect your child's last name,
address, phone number, photos, voice, or location.

### 3. How we use it
To provide the learning experience, screen content for age-appropriateness, alert
you to flagged moments, and give you **debriefs, progress tracking, and
homeschool records**. We **never** sell your child's information or show them
behavioral ads.

### 4. Who it's shared with
To generate replies, your child's messages are sent to our **AI provider
([Anthropic / Claude])**, which processes the data **on our behalf** and, under
our agreement, **does not use it to train its models**. ⚖️ Billing is handled by
**[Stripe]** and involves **no child data**.

> **If you choose "Pro / Transparency" mode:** you may instead connect **your own**
> AI provider account. Then your child's messages go to **your** provider under
> **your** account and that provider's terms, at your direction. We still apply
> our safety screening.

### 5. Your rights — anytime
From your parent dashboard you can **review** everything your child has shared
(including full transcripts), **download** it, **delete** it, and **withdraw your
consent** (which stops collection and deletes your child's information). Details
are in our [Privacy Policy]([PRIVACY POLICY URL]).

### 6. How long we keep it
Transcripts are kept on a rolling **12-month** window; if you delete a profile or
your account, your child's information is **hard-deleted within 30 days**. Full
details are in the [Privacy Policy]([PRIVACY POLICY URL]).

### 7. How you give consent
To verify you are the parent and give consent, we ask you to complete a **payment
card verification** as part of setting up your subscription or free trial. This is
a legally recognized way to confirm parental consent. ⚖️ *(Confirm card-based VPC
implementation with counsel.)*

If you do **not** complete consent within a reasonable time, we will delete the
information collected to seek your consent.
