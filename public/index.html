How Curio works

You stay in control. Curio is the safe, encouraging layer between your child and a world-class model.

1

Connect your model

Link your Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI account and pick the model you trust. Your key is encrypted and never shared with your child.

2

Set up each child

Add their grade and interests. Choose how much oversight you want — approve every new topic, or get a daily or weekly debrief.

3

They explore & build

Curio coaches from first principles — breaking big ideas into tiny steps, asking guiding questions, and celebrating effort to build real confidence.

4

You stay in the loop

Read the full transcript, approve pending topics, and get a plain-English debrief of what your child explored and where they lit up.

Safety rails, by design 🛡️

Defense in depth. No single mechanism is trusted on its own — and you set the guardrails.

🎚️

Age-tuned by grade

Vocabulary, topics, and reply length adapt to K–2, 3–5, or 6–8. Off-limits subjects are refused gently and redirected.

✅

Topic approval gates

For younger kids, every new project waits for your yes. Older kids get a daily or weekly brief — your call, per child.

🔎

Two-way screening

Every message in and out is scanned. Sensitive moments (sadness, safety) trigger caring responses and alert you.

📝

Full transcripts

Read everything your child and Curio said, anytime. Nothing is hidden from you.

📬

Parent debriefs

A warm, plain-English summary of topics explored, skills practiced, and anything to follow up on.

🔐

Your key, encrypted

Your provider key is AES-encrypted at rest and never exposed to your child or their device.
